Group — A (Multiple Choice Questions)   [10 × 1 = 10 Marks]
1. Which of the following is an irrational number?
A. $\sqrt{23}$
B. $\sqrt{225}$
C. $0.3799$
D. $7.\overline{478}$
2. Which of the following numbers is irrational?
A. $\sqrt{\frac{4}{9}}$
B. $\frac{\sqrt{1250}}{\sqrt{8}}$
C. $\sqrt{8}$
D. $\frac{\sqrt{24}}{\sqrt{6}}$
3. The product of two irrational numbers is:
A. always irrational
B. always rational
C. always an integer
D. sometimes rational & sometimes irrational
4. A rational number lying between $\sqrt{2}$ and $\sqrt{3}$ is:
A. $\frac{\sqrt{2}+\sqrt{3}}{2}$
B. $\sqrt{6}$
C. $1.6$
D. $1.9$
5. If $x = \frac{\sqrt{7}}{5}$ and $\frac{5}{x} = p\sqrt{7}$, then the value of $p$ is:
A. $\frac{7}{25}$
B. $\frac{25}{7}$
C. $\frac{7}{15}$
D. $\frac{15}{7}$
6. Which of the following is a true statement?
A. $\pi$ and $\frac{22}{7}$ are both rationals.
B. $\pi$ and $\frac{22}{7}$ are both irrationals.
C. $\pi$ is rational and $\frac{22}{7}$ is irrational.
D. $\pi$ is irrational and $\frac{22}{7}$ is rational.
7. If $\sqrt{2} = 1.414$, then the value of $\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$ is:
A. $0.075$
B. $0.75$
C. $0.705$
D. $7.05$
8. A rational number between $-3$ and $3$ is:
A. $0$
B. $-4.3$
C. $-3.4$
D. $1.101100110001\dots$
9. Every point on a number line represents:
A. a rational number
B. a natural number
C. an irrational number
D. a unique number
