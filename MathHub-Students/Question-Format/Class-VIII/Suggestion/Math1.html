Chapter 11. Percentage:
(i)
If the bamboo is $2\text{ metres}$ long and of which $38\text{ cm}$ is coloured with yellow, then what is the percentage of yellow colour in relation to the whole length of the bamboo?
(ii)
A train will go to Burdwan from Howrah station. The distance of chord line between Burdwan and Howrah is $85\text{ km}$. But the distance in the main line is $5\%$ more than chord line. Let's calculate the distance between Howrah and Burdwan in the main line by the method of proportion.
(iii)
The income of Amina Bibi was increased by $20\%$ and later decreased by $20\%$. Let's work out the total change of percentage of her income.
(iv)
The length of a rectangle is increased by $15\%$ and the breadth is reduced by $15\%$. Let's work out in percentage the increase or decrease of area.
(v)
In a legislative election, $80\%$ voters cast their votes and the winning candidate got $65\%$ of the cast votes. Let's work out the percentage of total voters who supported him.
(vi)
The price of sugar has increased by $20\%$. Let's work out the percentage of decrease in consumption of sugar if the monthly expenses of sugar remain same.
(vii)
The length of each arm of a square was increased by $10\%$. Let's work out the increase of percentage of area by the Rule of Three.
(viii)
Uma Devi of Pahalampur has used high-yielding seeds in her fields. As a result, the production of paddy has increased by $20\%$, but the cost of production of paddy has increased by $25\%$. Earlier she used to get the production worth $\text{Rs. } 1560$ by spending $\text{Rs. } 600$. Let's work out how much she will earn more now using high-yielding seeds.
(ix)
Niyamat uncle of Faridpur has used high-yielding paddy seed in his field. For this the production of paddy has increased by $35\%$ but the cost of cultivation has increased by $35\%$. Let's work out the present profit of Niyamat uncle in comparison with the previous return of $\text{Rs. } 1220$ by investing $\text{Rs. } 450$ in the same field.
